PRIVACY NOTICE (Art. 13 GDPR)

App: Race Engineer (beta). Last updated: November 2026.

1. CONTROLLER

Raffaele Lo Savio Pagano (developer name on Google Play: Ralos Lab), ralos.business@gmail.com. To exercise your rights write to this address.

2. DATA WE PROCESS

  • Account: email, nickname, country (optional), creation date. The sign-in system also records standard technical data (for example IP address and access times).
  • Sessions: GPS points with time and speed, the phone's accelerometer and gyroscope data, OBD/CAN channels if you connect the car, laps and sector times, circuit, start and end times, hardware type, app version.
  • Vehicle: make, model, tyres, drivetrain, power and weight of the vehicle you chose. We do not collect licence plate or chassis number.
  • Personal tracks: name and gate points of the tracks you create.
  • Acceptance of the terms: version, date of acceptance and of withdrawal.
  • Location: the app uses location only while you use the lap timer and telemetry, with a visible notification, and does not ask for the background location permission.
  • Video and audio: if you turn on session video recording, the video (and the audio, if you grant the microphone) stays on your phone and is not sent to us.
  • Bluetooth and USB: only used to connect the OBD adapter and the GPS receiver; we do not read or send data from other devices.

3. WHICH SESSIONS WE SEND

Only sessions recorded on an official circuit, or on a personal track with at least one gate within 5 km of an official circuit. The others stay on your phone only.

4. PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES

a) Providing the account, the session archive and the beta features (Art. 6.1.b, contract). Without this data we cannot offer the service. This is why, during the beta, using u-blox and OBD requires the account.

b) Checking circuits, preventing abuse and improving the app using the sessions sent (Art. 6.1.f, legitimate interest). Processing is internal only. You can object at any time by deleting your data or writing to the Controller.

c) Personalised coaching (Art. 6.1.a, optional consent). Only if you give the specific consent, we will use the traces of your sessions to analyse your driving style (braking, lines, acceleration, consistency between laps) and build coaching tailored to you, with models developed by us. The feature is not active yet: it will arrive in future versions, and the sessions you send in the meantime serve to prepare it. We do not use external AI providers. You can withdraw consent at any time: after that your sessions are no longer used for this purpose. Models already built are not necessarily redone.

Who sees what: your sessions, laps and tracks are seen only by us. Your nickname, and your country if you give one, are visible to other users with an account. We do not use the data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we take no decision that affects you based solely on automated processing. If in future we show data to other users (for example in leaderboards), we will do it only with a new notice and, where needed, a new consent from you.

5. RECIPIENTS

Supabase Inc. (database, authentication and file storage), data processor. Data is on servers in Frankfurt (Germany). As Supabase is a US company, access from outside the EU can only happen with the safeguards of Art. 46 GDPR (standard contractual clauses).

Sign-in email provider: Google LLC, certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Google Maps: the app shows maps with Google Maps; Google may process technical device data under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

6. RETENTION

Sessions and tracks stay until you delete them or until the beta ends. When the beta closes we warn you 30 days ahead. Then we delete the sessions, or we offer you to continue with a new notice and a new consent. We do not keep your sessions beyond this term without telling you. “Delete my data” erases sessions and tracks and leaves email and nickname; “Delete my account” (Settings → Account beta) immediately erases email, nickname, consents and the date you accepted the terms as well. While the account exists we keep the acceptance date so we can prove it.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Arts. 15-22 GDPR), and withdrawal of the personal coach consent at any time. You can delete your data or your account from the app (Settings → Account beta), or write to ralos.business@gmail.com from the address you registered with. You can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante: garanteprivacy.it).

8. WHETHER PROVIDING DATA IS REQUIRED

Providing data is optional. Without an account the app only works as a lap timer with the phone GPS.

9. AGE

The service is for adults only.

10. UPDATES

When the beta ends, or when we introduce the purchase and new features involving other users, we will update this notice and tell you.

BETA PROGRAMME TERMS

1. NATURE AND DURATION

The app is experimental and free. It may have errors in times and telemetry, and data may be lost or reset. The beta lasts 6 months from launch, until 1 June 2027. We may extend, change or close it, warning you by email at least 30 days ahead.

2. WHAT WILL BE PAID AND THE TESTER BENEFIT

During the beta all features are unlocked. When the beta ends the lap timer with the phone GPS stays free, while using the external u-blox GPS receiver and the OBD/CAN interface will need the purchase of the base version of the app through Google Play, with your account tied to the purchase. Taking part does not oblige you to buy anything.

You get the base version for free if, during the beta: (i) you keep your account active; (ii) you send sessions in at least 4 of the 6 months; (iii) you send at least 3 pieces of feedback through the channel we will tell you. 3. USE ON TRACK

The app must be used only on circuits closed to traffic. Do not touch the phone or the devices while driving. Set it up with the vehicle stopped. The data is amateur and informative and is not approved for competitions.
